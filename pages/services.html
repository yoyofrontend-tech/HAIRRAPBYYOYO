    Booking information & terms

    Salon visits are by prior appointment. A non-refundable ₹5,000 advance is required for bookings with YOYO Sir; send a payment screenshot as confirmation. Additional hair treatment charges may apply for extra length, volume or growth.

    Makeup lounge bookings require full advance payment and are non-refundable if cancelled by the client. A full refund applies if the salon cancels. Appointments between 5–9 AM cost an additional ₹2,000 per person. Late appointments are handled on a first-come, first-served basis.

    Bridal hair trials take place 2–4 weeks before the function; a half-face bridal makeup trial costs ₹5,000. Hair extensions and real flowers are included, and hair accessories are on deposit and must be returned within 48–72 hours. Damage or delays may be deducted from the deposit. Arrive with a clean face and washed hair; removal charges may apply otherwise. Only the bride or groom, as applicable, may enter the makeup lounge. Destination work is priced on consultation.
