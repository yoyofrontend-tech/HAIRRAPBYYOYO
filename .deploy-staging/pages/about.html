THE JOURNEY

From a love of learning to a space for self-expression.

After returning from the UK, YOYO discovered hairdressing and began learning from his barber. He practised with free haircuts at people’s homes, asking for feedback as he developed his craft.

His education continued with Vipul Chudasama and advanced training with Mounir in Dubai. In 2022, he established Hair Rap by YOYO in Ahmedabad.
