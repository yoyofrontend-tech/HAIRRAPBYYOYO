About the salon

HAIR RAP BY YOYO is a hair and beauty salon in Bodakdev, Ahmedabad. YOYO opened the salon in 2022.

As founder and creative director, YOYO works alongside co-founders Anuj and Garima and a team of hair and beauty artists. We start each visit with a consultation about your style and routine.
